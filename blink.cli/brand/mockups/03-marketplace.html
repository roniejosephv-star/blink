the contract

Stays true to contributions.

Three commitments Tinkr makes to every plugin author and every user. We wrote them down so they're enforceable, not aspirational.

01

You own your plugin.

Source lives in your GitHub repo, always. MIT or your choice of license. We cannot lock you out, take your plugin down, or change your license. If Tinkr disappears, your plugin still installs from git.

02

70% of every sale is yours.

Tinkr takes 30% to cover registry, payment, and the curation queue. The rest is yours. Payouts monthly, transparent ledger, no minimum threshold. If your plugin goes paid → free, you keep the recognition.

03

You control your code.

No "AI rewrite" of your plugin. No silent updates. Every version is a git tag, every changelog entry is yours to write. If the user is offline, the plugin still works. If you delete your plugin, we delete the marketplace listing within 24 hours.

If you've written a Tinkr plugin that solves a real problem, the creator program is how you ship it, get paid, and grow a user base. We curate; you build.
