The Tinkr trace network.

Seventeen bright traces in PCB style — 45° chamfered corners, never square. Hollow pins at the ends. Filled junctions at the crossings. Ten dynamic nodes flow along curved Bezier paths through the lines, like data packets on a real chip. No straight-line motion. No Manhattan corners.
